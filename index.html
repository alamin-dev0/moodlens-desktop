      Camera permission and privacy

      Camera permission: unknown

      • All analysis runs in your browser with WebAssembly/WebGL. Frames are never uploaded, recorded or stored.
      • Only the model files and libraries are downloaded from public CDNs on load.
      • History holds scores and timestamps only, and stays in memory unless you turn on “Remember history” in Settings.
      • To remove camera access entirely, use your browser's site-permission controls (the lock icon in the address bar).
